You are helping me brainstorm lore and design for my game. Please come up with ideas, not code.

## The game
A pixel-art roguelike card game (Phaser). A run is 3 acts of 15 floors; you pick a road (location) for each act. Fights are played on a board of face-down cards: enemies, loot, traps. You fight with weapon and armor cards from a bag, and merge two equal cards to raise their rarity (common → uncommon → rare → epic → legendary; two legendaries now add a star, up to 3, and a starred card holds 3 sigils instead of 2). Stories (events) appear between fights. My writing voice is dry and situation-first: say what is in the room and what happens, no grand aphorisms, no "ChatGPT" flourishes.

## Sigils and stamps
A sigil is a small ability on a weapon or armor card (max 2 per card). They come from **Sigil Rooms**: a stone stamp with an animal figurine on its base stands on a dais; you drag a card onto the slab, the stamp slams down and the card gets the sigil. Today the 5 stamps are animal families: lion (might), turtle (shell), toad (wealth), cobra (venom), deer (grace).

The new rule I want: **each location has its own stamp**, and its Sigil Rooms give sigils that fit that location's lore. Locations are loosely themed by an element or nature force, but the tie can be loose; what matters is a clear identity.

Current player sigils (40): venom, sunder, daze, crushing, cutpurse, leech, fleetfoot, desperate, outnumbered, merciless, ambush, unscathed, honed, ironclad, fireball, sapping, warding, mirror, regenerate, devouring, smoke, tempered, hunter, combo, tandem, packrat, kinship, twinSocket, keen, ransom, bounty, bloodlust, tsunami, warcry, riposte, mending, secondWind, charm, scout, frost. (Plus Thorns from the Briar Room and Crystal Brood from the Crystal Lair.)
