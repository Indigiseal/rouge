# Brassfair

The month of false lanterns, cheap wonders, and deals that smell of brass. In this turn danger often smiles and offers to sell you something wonderful.

## Theme

Cheerful wrongness — lanterns, deals, and machines that should not smile

## Location

### The Brassfair Grounds

**The Brassfair Grounds** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Faircutter]], [[Clockwork Barker]], [[Brass Imp]], [[Prize Rifleman]], [[Fortune Lobber]].  
Boss: [[The Ringmaster]].

## Features

- Carnival logic: junk, fortunes, illegal machines.
- Brass, cogs, and smiling merchants who should not exist.
- Natural home for the music-box lane's tone.

- [[08 Brassfair/Events/The Brass Wizard|The Brass Wizard]] — A coin-slot fortune machine behind carnival curtains. Fortunes, card swaps, jams, or a machine that wakes with human eyes.
