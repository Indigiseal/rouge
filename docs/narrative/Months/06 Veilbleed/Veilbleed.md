# Veilbleed

The month when the boundary between living and dead thins. Whispers linger longer than they should, and some shadows stop belonging to bodies.

## Theme

Thin veil — living and dead leak into each other

## Location

### The Bleeding Veil

**The Bleeding Veil** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Lost Soul]], [[Hollow Mourner]], [[Veil Ripper]], [[Ink Wraith]], [[Grave Cantor]].  
Boss: [[Soul Eater]].

## Features

- The veil thins: souls, moths, ink, and stolen names.
- Magic feels hungry rather than helpful.
