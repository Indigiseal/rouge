# Thornwake

The month of thorns, beasts, and restless woods. The forest is wrapped in living spikes: roots push into stone, brush crawls toward roads, and animals no longer fear fire.

## Theme

Living wilds — growth that refuses to stay outside the walls

## Location

### The Briar March

**The Briar March** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Wolf]], [[Thorn Ent]], [[Thorn Sprite]], [[Spore Archer]], [[Thorn Fairy]].  
Boss: [[The Green Warden]].

## Features

- Living growth invades roads and ruins.
- Beasts lose their fear of fire and settlements.
- False kindness may hide in green places.

## Events

- [[01 Thornwake/Events/The Briar Room|The Briar Room]] — A room walled in living briars. Offer gear to be thorn-touched, cut through, burn them, or leave.
- [[01 Thornwake/Events/The Too-Nice Room|The Too-Nice Room]] — A corridor room that should not exist: bed, tea, flowers. Rest, leave, or catch the fairy who staged the kindness.
