# Stormhatch

The month of thunder, peals, and things that leave their shells too soon. In this turn the sky does not merely shine — it incubates.

## Theme

Sky incubation — thunder, nests, and things born too early

## Location

### The Hatchspires

**The Hatchspires** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Storm Chick]], [[Nest Raider]], [[Thunder Beetle]], [[Storm Screecher]], [[Egg Sniper]].  
Boss: [[The Nestmother]].
