# Boneflood

The month of old bones. In this turn the earth gives back what it was supposed to keep.

## Theme

Unearthed dead — the ground returns what it was meant to keep

## Location

### The Ossuary Fields

**The Ossuary Fields** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Skeleton]], [[Bone Heap]], [[Crypt Warden]], [[Skeleton Archer]], [[Skull Lobber]].  
Boss: [[Giant Skeleton]].

## Features

- Crypts, ossuaries, and broken monuments open.
- The dead are not quiet; they are inventory.
- Sacred or cursed relics feel closer to the surface.

## Events

- [[04 Boneflood/Events/The Reliquary|The Reliquary]] — Lit cases of relics. Search, smash, or leave — tied to enchanting a weapon you already carry.
- [[04 Boneflood/Events/The Old Drill Room|The Old Drill Room]] — A ruined training hall that reacts to companion cards — upgrade storm chick, skeleton, or another follower.

## Soft fits

- [[12 Spherefall/Events/The Screaming Head|The Screaming Head]] _(soft)_ — A buried stone head mid-scream. Feed it a card, risk your arm, or socket a gem for a rune.
