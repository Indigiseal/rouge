# Silkdeep

The month of caves, damp stone, and silk. Whatever preferred the cracks in other seasons comes closer to the surface.

## Theme

Depth and silk — what lived in cracks climbs toward the light

## Location

### The Silkdeep Caves

**The Silkdeep Caves** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Spider]], [[Cave Crawler]], [[Web-Bound Skeleton]], [[Spitting Spider]], [[Silkslinger]].  
Boss: [[Spider Queen]].

- Depth rises: caves, wells, and tunnels feel nearer.

- Quiet rooms often hide something still digesting.
