# Silkdeep

The month of caves, damp stone, and silk. Whatever preferred the cracks in other seasons comes closer to the surface.

## Theme

Depth and silk — what lived in cracks climbs toward the light

## Location

### The Silkdeep Caves

**The Silkdeep Caves** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Spider]], [[Cave Crawler]], [[Silk Husk]], [[Stinger Scorpion]], [[Silkslinger]].  
Boss: [[Spider Queen]].

## Features

- Depth rises: caves, wells, and tunnels feel nearer.
- Silk, venom, and trapped prey define the threat.
- Quiet rooms often hide something still digesting.

## Events

- [[02 Silkdeep/Events/The Slimy Prison|The Slimy Prison]] — A gelatinous cube holds a regenerating skeleton mage. Free him as companion, end him for an amulet, or steal the charm and leave him trapped.
- [[02 Silkdeep/Events/The Silk Cache|The Silk Cache]] — A chamber of cocoons and abandoned arms. Leave, crack them open in a fight, or burn the nest with Fireball.
