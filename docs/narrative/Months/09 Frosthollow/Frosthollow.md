# Frosthollow

The month of cold, emptiness, and hungry silence. Sound dies faster, and the world looks carved from pale stone.

## Theme

Cold hunger — silence, pale space, and appetite without warmth

## Location

### Frosthollow

**Frosthollow** is the signature place of this month.

Roster encountered here: [[Ice Wight]], [[Hunger Wolf]], [[Pale Stalker]], [[Frost Archer]], [[Hail Thrower]].  
Boss: [[The Hollow Voice]].
