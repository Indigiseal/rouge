# Music Box Chain

Optional cross-month lane (not required for the macro-plot). Tone leans [[08 Brassfair/Brassfair|Brassfair]] and [[10 Stormhatch/Stormhatch|Stormhatch]], but progress must survive month changes.

## Sequence

1. [[08 Brassfair/Events/The Broken Music Box|The Broken Music Box]] — opener
2. [[10 Stormhatch/Events/Monster Bird Nest|Monster Bird Nest]] — cog / egg
3. [[03 Tollroad/Events/Goblin Engineer|Goblin Engineer]] — repair
4. [[10 Stormhatch/Events/The Egg Hatches|The Egg Hatches]] — companion (if egg taken)

## Design rule

Weight the encounters toward matching months, but never soft-lock the lane because the calendar moved on.
