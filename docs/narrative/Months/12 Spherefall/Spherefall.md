# Spherefall

The month of fallen sky-stone. Craters, dark glass, and star-iron shards mark the fields — debris of old impacts, not a countdown to the year's end.

## Theme

Fallen sky-stone — meteor scars, crystal debris, and things that hatch from impact glass
