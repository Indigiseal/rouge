# Design Decisions

Captured from team discussion. Short, locked-for-now notes.

## Encounters need not interconnect

Roadside vignettes can stand alone. **Boss speeches** on the true road are the exception: they chain. See [[The True Path]].

## Path pick, not calendar

No month rotation. Each act: choose one of three locations. Completing any of them advances the act.

## True road is a signal, not a gate

One true pick per act: **Tollroad → Brassfair → Starfold**.

Wrong country: after-boss card ("this was not the road"), then the next act's three doors anyway. Starfold is never locked behind Tollroad or Brassfair.

## The nine

| Act | Locations |
|---|---|
| 1 | Thornwake (forest), Silkdeep (caves), **Tollroad** (goblin mile, true) |
| 2 | Boneflood (ossuary), **Brassfair** (dark carnival, true), Duskhold (vampire castle) |
| 3 | Mirrorwane (mirror palace), Spherefall (craters), **Starfold** (Inner Sky, true) |

See [[Locations Index]].

## Act sets strangeness, not difficulty

Act 1 recognisable, act 2 owned-and-hungry, act 3 the sky's own country. The three roads of one act share that register. Never reshuffle a road between acts to tune difficulty — that is bands and floors. See [[Tone and Fantasy]].

## Wrong Act 3 still finishes a run

Mirrorwane / Spherefall grant a lesser answer and full meta XP. They are not fake victories.

## Discovery is not tracked

No codex, no journal, no mark on the choice screen for a road already walked. What the player learned about the true chain lives with the player, not in a save file.

## Replay beyond the map

The road pick stops being a real choice once the chain is known. That is accepted. Long-tail replay is the job of character meta and the planned village layer (plus possible seasonal act modifiers), not of the nine roads. See [[Replayability]].

## Location combat roster

Each location: **3 melee**, **2 ranged**, **1 boss**.

## Enemy power bands + archetypes

Bands × archetypes. Location picks who; floor picks how strong. See [[Enemy Power]].
