# Design Decisions

Captured from team discussion. Short, locked-for-now notes.

## Encounters need not interconnect

Dialogue / vignettes can stand alone. Month placement gives them a home; they do not all have to reference each other.

## Branching shelved

No three-path act opens for now. Replayability is calendar rotation, not route choice.

## Run = three consecutive months

Window slides around the 12-month circle:

`1-2-3`, `2-3-4`, …, `11-12-1`, `12-1-2`.

First playthroughs should feel new because the starting triple keeps changing.

## No year-end special month

The calendar loops. Finishing month 12 and entering month 1 is ordinary. **Spherefall** is a debris / impact-glass face, not "the sphere draws near" and not a yearly apocalypse beat.

## Month combat roster

Each month face targets: **3 melee enemies**, **2 ranged enemies**, **1 boss**.

## Enemy power bands + archetypes

Ordinary enemies: shared floor **bands** (SoT in `docs/BALANCE.md`) × **archetype** multipliers.  
Monster identity holds role + feature without strength numbers. Month picks the roster; floor picks the band.
