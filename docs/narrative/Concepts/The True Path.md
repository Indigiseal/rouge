# The True Path

Choosing a location is **picking a road**, not spinning a calendar. Every act still offers three countries. Completing any of them **advances the act**. One of the three is **the true road** — the Waystar's road, the rumor that actually points at [[The Magus]].

Wrong country: you still win the act. The game says so, plainly, then opens the next three doors. No softlock. No hidden lock on later picks.

## The chain (locked)

| Act | True location | Who speaks | What they give you |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Tollroad]] | [[Goblin King]] | His kingdom taxes in the name of a **new deity**. He did not invent it. Priests from a night-fair taught the goblins a name and a seal. He points, boasting, at **the carnival**. |
| 2 | [[Brassfair]] | [[The Ringmaster]] | The dark carnival **sells that deity** as the greatest wonder: tickets, relics, a brass sky. The Ringmaster is priest and con-man. He says the tent is a porch. The house is **inside the sphere**. |
| 3 | [[Starfold]] | [[The Magus]] | The deity itself. The Inner Sky. End of the true road. |

True expedition: **Tollroad → Brassfair → Starfold**.

The Magus is still a stub as a person. As a *name the world already uses*, they are locked: goblins tax for them, the fair sells them, Starfold is their house.

## After the act boss

A short card. Not a lore dump.

**True**

- Tollroad: *The King named a god. The Waystar burns toward the lanterns.*
- Brassfair: *The fair sold the sky. The house is inward.*
- Starfold: the ending (write with the Magus).

**Wrong (Act 1)**

- Thornwake: *The forest kept you. It was not the road.*
- Silkdeep: *The silk is quiet. The Waystar still points elsewhere.*

**Wrong (Act 2)**

- Boneflood: *The dead have no church. This was not the house.*
- Duskhold: *The castle drinks. It does not keep the sphere.*

**Wrong (Act 3)**

- Mirrorwane: *You fought a copy. The Magus was not in the glass.*
- Spherefall: *You stood in the wound. You did not step through.*

Wrong Act 3 still **counts as a finished run** for meta. The card is a lesser answer, not a fake win screen that withholds XP.

## If the player skipped a true country

The next true boss still works.

- You never walked the Mile: the Ringmaster still sells the god. He may mock the missing taxmen ("the goblins learned the name last; you learned it here").
- You never walked the fair: Starfold does not require a ticket. The Magus does not owe the player a recap. Optional line if Brassfair was cleared this run.

Do not gate Starfold behind Tollroad or Brassfair.

## What this is not

- Not three exclusive campaigns.
- Not a fail state.
- Roadside events still need not form a novel. See [[Encounter Philosophy]]. The **boss speeches** carry the chain.
