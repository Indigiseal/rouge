# Day and Floor

In Evershift, a floor is not abstract depth. It is **one day on the road**.

That translation gives the story rhythm:

- morning reads as a new floor;
- progress reads as physically survived days;
- rising difficulty reads as the accumulated pressure of the month.

## Core formula

- **1 floor = 1 day**
- **15 floors = 1 month**
- **45 floors = 3 months of travel**

This is the spine that binds lore to game structure.

## Why it matters

- turns the run into a journey, not only a clear;
- enables day and month transitions in writing;
- gives narrative meaning to the numbers 15 and 45.
