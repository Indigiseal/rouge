# Tone and Fantasy

## Fantasy

**I walk a road pointed out by a fallen star, through the months of a broken sky.**

The hero is not merely surviving. They keep moving toward a place no one else can reach with confidence.

## Tone

The world's tone rests on three pillars:

1. **Foreboding** — the sky has already changed, and everyone lives after that.
2. **Strangeness** — each month-face makes the world a little wrong.
3. **Stubbornness** — the hero walks anyway.

This is not an epic about a chosen one, and not grimdark about the end of everything. It is a story about a world forced to live by an alien rhythm, and about a person who decides to walk all the way to the source of that rhythm.
