# The Magus

The Magus began as a human court magician in [[Silkdeep]], long before the
Second Moon rose. He read branching futures in the webs of an exotic spider
collection and advised the local baron on harvests, trade, credit, marriage,
and rivals.

Then an unknown whisper told him that the rise of the [[The Second Moon|Second Moon]]
would begin the **Age of Confluence**: the promised time when all worlds would
merge into one and a ruler capable of great works would come to claim them.
The magician believed it. He became the earthly deputy and right hand of that
unseen higher power. He did not merely predict the Second Moon: he helped bring
about its rise.

## Locked for now

- He is the former court magician of the human palace now called [[Silkdeep]].
- He heard the unknown whisper before the Second Moon rose.
- He believes the Second Moon begins the **Age of Confluence**, when all worlds
  merge and a coming ruler gains the power to accomplish great works.
- He serves that still-unknown higher evil as its deputy and right hand.
- He both foretold and helped cause the rise of the Second Moon.
- [[Starfold]] is now his demesne — the Inner Sky.
- He is the boss of that Act 3 location and the game's principal active antagonist.
- Goblins and the Night Fair may worship or sell the Magus as the visible face
  of the power above him; their theology need not distinguish master from deputy.
- He is *not* required on Mirrorwane, Spherefall, Duskhold, or Boneflood finales.
- The player must not learn that Silkdeep's court magician became the Magus
  during the first Silkdeep visit. That identity is a later recontextualization.

## Not locked

Name before becoming the Magus; face and voice; the identity and nature of the
whispering ruler; whether the Magus is loyal, deceived, or planning to supplant
his master; the exact ritual that raised the Second Moon.
