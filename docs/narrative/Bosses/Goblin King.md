# Goblin King

A classic **goblin king** — parody sovereign of a road that never asked for one. He did not inherit a realm; he stamped one onto the Mile: tariffs, "guards," a throne of failed wagons.

He also **found a religion**. Carnival priests came down the Mile with lanterns, a name, and a seal. The King taxes in that name and thinks himself holy. In the act-end speech he boasts of the **new deity** and of the **night fair** where it is sold as a wonder. That is the true-path handoff to [[Brassfair]]. See [[The True Path]].
