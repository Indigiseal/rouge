# Goblin King

A classic **goblin king** — parody sovereign of a road that never asked for one. He did not inherit a realm; he stamped one onto the Mile: tariffs, "guards," a throne of failed wagons.

He also **found a religion**. Carnival priests came down the Mile with lanterns, a name, and a seal. The King taxes in that name and thinks himself holy. With his last breath he whispers, **"Magus... you promised..."** He does not understand the dark pendant hidden beneath his gold: after his death it awakens and projects an unknown figure. The hero can infer that this is probably the Magus, but at this point the name is all they know. The apparition's promise to expose the world's hidden treasures is the true-path handoff beyond Tollroad. See [[The True Path]].
