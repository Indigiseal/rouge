# Goblin King

A classic **goblin king** — parody sovereign of a road that never asked for one. He did not inherit a realm; he stamped one onto the Mile: tariffs, "guards," and a throne built from whatever wagons failed to pay. The finale of Tollroad; choices at [[03 Tollroad/Events/Toll Collectors|Toll Collectors]] should still be felt here.
