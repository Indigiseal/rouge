# The Ringmaster

Showman of the **Night Fair**. Brass, cloth, a smile that is work. He is priest and con-man of the same deity the goblins tax for: he **sells the sky** as tickets and relics. The tent, he says, is a porch. The house is inside the sphere — [[Starfold]].

If the hero cleared Tollroad this run, he already knows the tax-name. If not, he still preaches; he may mention goblins who learned the word last.

After the fight (true road): *The fair sold the sky. The house is inward.* See [[The True Path]].
