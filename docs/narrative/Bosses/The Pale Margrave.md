# The Pale Margrave

Lord of [[Duskhold]]. A vampire sovereign in a real castle: title, court, tithe, and a hunger that has outlived the family name.

Not a priest of [[The Magus]]. The Margrave drinks the valley. The sphere is a rival light in the window.
