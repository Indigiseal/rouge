# The Village

The hero is not a knight, a chosen bloodline, or a city savior by office. They are **a villager** who left home to find the source of the Second Moon's law — the power that pools villains into the nine countries and tries to make calamity ordinary.

Older notes called this settlement [[The City]]. That name still appears in rumor (a "city" under two moons). On the ground it is a village: gates, fields, people who remember the old seasons. From those gates the Near Road still splits three ways — woods, caves, the Mile.

## Why this villager leaves

They leave because waiting means watching the world fill with things that should not walk it. The [[The Waystar|Waystar]] points through danger, not away from it. Someone has to walk. This someone is ordinary, and that is the point.

They are looking for **the source** — whatever sent the sphere, whatever summons the villains, whatever [[The Far Place]] will name. The true chain (goblin tax-god → night fair → [[The Magus]]) is how the world already talks about that source. The villager does not have to know the chain on the first morning.

## What the village is

The village is:

- home, and the stake (ordinary life the sphere is rewriting);
- where the Waystar **returns** the body after death on the road;
- where each new expedition chooses the first of three Near Roads;
- the people who watch the hero come back, and decide whether to believe.

They cannot walk the farther countries with the hero. What they can do is **keep faith**, and turn that faith into work: timber, iron, a roof, a forge. See [[Villager Support]].
