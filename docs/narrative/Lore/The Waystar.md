# The Waystar

## Origin

When the Second Moon first appeared beside the ordinary moon, a stone fell from the sky that same night. It did not shatter village walls or burn a crater the size of a square. It fell quietly, almost without sound, as if the world had already prepared a place for it.

Later, people named the stone the **Waystar**.

## What it does

The Waystar always points in one direction. Not north, not toward a temple, not toward home. Its bearing answers to no compass, road, or human border.

It leads to [[The Far Place]].

It does not pick the three Near Roads for the walker. It only refuses to point *away*. The choice of country is the walker's.

No one knows what it remembers:

- the place of its own fall;
- the path to the Second Moon's source;
- a command from whatever sent it down;
- or the only route by which the world might still be repaired.

In [[Starfold]] it is said to be loud. It fell from that kind of sky.

## Bond with the villager

The Waystar did not pick a prophesied savior. It will not **release** the ordinary person who keeps walking. Until the road is finished, it returns them to life whenever they die along the way.

This return does not feel like a church miracle. It feels more like a corrected line: the world rolls the body back to the [[The Village|Village]], and demands another attempt at the nine countries. The neighbours see that return. Their faith — [[Villager Support]] — is how the village answers, not how the stone levels up.

## Why it matters

The Waystar:

- gives the walker a clear goal;
- explains the roguelite loop;
- binds every location into one shared journey;
- turns death from an ending into a return.
