# The Far Place

The Far Place is the end of [[The Path]], the only destination the [[The Waystar|Waystar]] indicates without error.

No one knows what it truly is.

## Legends

People speak of the Far Place as:

- an observatory built before history;
- a wound in the sky hidden under stone;
- a gate through which the Second Moon entered;
- a throne where someone keeps the sphere on a leash;
- a place where the world can still be asked why it changed.

None of these versions is confirmed. All of them agree on one thing: if an answer exists, it is there.

## Narrative role

The Far Place is useful precisely because it is unfinished. It:

- keeps a distant goal beyond the horizon;
- gives the Path meaning;
- lets the team avoid locking the full meta-plot too early;
- promises a major reveal in the late game.
