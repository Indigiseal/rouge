# The City

The City is what must be saved, even if it does not yet know how.

It lives under two moons, as under two laws: the old one people still remember, and the new one that already rules. Each new month changes what the townsfolk fear most. Once it is the beast at the gates. Next it is silk in the wells. Next it is people on the roads who have ceased to be people just enough for a toll to become a form of hunting.

## Why the hero leaves

The hero does not leave for glory or loot. They leave because:

- the Waystar points the road only for them;
- waiting means allowing months to become normal;
- if the source is never reached, the City will live from calamity to calamity until it forgets that time was ever peaceful.

## What the City gives the story

The City is:

- the emotional reason for the journey;
- the measure of the stakes;
- a point the mind can return to between acts;
- an image of ordinary life the Second Moon is gradually rewriting.
