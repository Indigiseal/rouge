# Death and Return

In Evershift, dying on the road is not the end of the story. If the villager falls, the [[The Waystar|Waystar]] returns them to life **in the [[The Village|Village]]**, at the start of a new expedition.

This is not consequence-free immortality. The nine countries are still there. The Path did not wait. Someone else may have taken the Mile, or the caves, or the Inner Sky, and not come back.

The village **sees the return**. How far the rumor reached — which day, which named boss — is what fills them with faith that this walker might be the one who finds the source. That faith is [[Villager Support]]. With it they raise buildings for the next leaving.

## What this gives the story

- explains the roguelite loop without a calendar that advances for you;
- makes death a homecoming to people, not a skip to a menu;
- binds defeat to replayability (pick the three roads again) and to meta (the village works while you sleep it off);
- keeps the hero ordinary: they did not earn a miracle; the stone rolled them home, and the neighbours decided to help.

## Emotional meaning

Each return sounds less like "try again" and more like:

> You did not finish the road. We still think you might. Choose.
