# Silk Husk

A victim wrapped until the cocoon became a walking shell. Arms and legs jut from dense silk; the head is sealed inside, marked only by two cold **blue eyes** that glow in the dark and will not release a stare. While it stands, the Path's attention locks — nothing else on the board may be struck.

Replaces the old draft "Web-Bound Skeleton."

## Feature

`taunt` (**Provocation**) — while this enemy is alive and revealed, the player **cannot attack any other enemy** on the board (melee or ranged). Potions, reveals, and non-attack actions still work. Ends when the Husk falls.
