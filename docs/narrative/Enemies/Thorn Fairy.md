# Thorn Fairy

She was once an ordinary forest fairy. Now living thorn-vine coils her like a harness — leaf and briar where cloth should be — and the growth seems to steer her will. The kindness is gone from her face; what remains is bitterness aimed at anyone on the Path. In her hand a wand that is only a living branch, and from its tip fly barbed thorns when she strikes. Each turn she vanishes behind her own veil, card-face then card-back, as if the briar itself will not let the world keep hold of her.

Formerly drafted as "Briar Slinger."

## Feature

`veil_flip` — each of her turns she flips: face-down (cannot be attacked, does not strike) or face-up (strikes, then veils again). Face-down she wakes without striking that turn.
