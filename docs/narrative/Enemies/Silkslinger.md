# Silkslinger

A small, quick cave spider that fights with strand, not fang. From the dark it spatters sticky silk onto whatever you meant to use next — a weapon, a potion, a charm — and for a heartbeat that card is useless under webbing.

## Feature

`web_hand` — on attack, **webs one random card in the player's hand** for **1 turn** (visual silk overlay; card cannot be used). If the webbed card is the **only usable weapon** and the player has no other weapon to swing, that attack action is skipped the same way as having no weapon left.
