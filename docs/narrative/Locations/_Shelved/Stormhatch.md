# Stormhatch

> [!warning] Shelved
> Not one of the nine Path locations. Sky-hatching tone moves to Spherefall and Starfold. Nest / egg beats stay in the Music Box sequence. See [[Locations Index]].

The country of thunder, peals, and things that leave their shells too soon. Here the sky does not merely shine — it incubates.
