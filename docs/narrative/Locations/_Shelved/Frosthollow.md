# Frosthollow

> [!warning] Shelved
> Not one of the nine Path locations. Cold empty dead overlaps Boneflood and Veilbleed. Quiet Crossroads stays the any-location fallback. See [[Locations Index]].

The country of cold, emptiness, and hungry silence. Sound dies faster, and the world looks carved from pale stone.

## Theme

Cold hunger — silence, pale space, and appetite without warmth
