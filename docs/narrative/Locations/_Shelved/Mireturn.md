# Mireturn

> [!warning] Shelved
> Not one of the nine Path locations. Bog overlaps Silkdeep and Boneflood. Notes kept for salvage. See [[Locations Index]].

The country of standing water, fog, and slow decay. Everything feels heavier, wetter, and a little closer to spoil.

## Theme

Stagnant rot — water that will not flow and things that will not stay sunk
