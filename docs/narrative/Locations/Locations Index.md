# Locations Index

Nine **roads**. The player picks one per act. Completing any road advances the act. **One road per act is true** — see [[The True Path]].

True chain: **Tollroad → Brassfair → Starfold** (goblin tax-god → dark carnival of that god → the Magus's house).

Roster target per location: **3 melee + 2 ranged + 1 boss**.

Layout: each road has a folder `Locations/<Name>/` with its note and an `Events/` subfolder. Encounters owned by no road live in `_Shared/`; retired faces in `_Shelved/`.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–15 | Near country. Forest, cave, or a goblin-taxed mile. | [[Thornwake]] · [[Silkdeep]] · [[Tollroad]] **(true)** |

27 combinations. Wrong road still finishes the act; the after-boss card says it was not the Waystar's road.

## Act 1 — Near country

### [[Thornwake]] — The Briar March (wrong)

A **forest that ate the old trade road**. A day's ride from the City the packed dirt disappears under briar. Wolves with thorn through the hide hunt in packs. Walking trees (small, fairy-tale ents) hit back through the blade. Vine-hydras ignore arrows. Sporelings lob glowing orbs. Angry fairies bound in vine flicker in and out of sight. The **Green Warden** is the forest's keeper: a tall ent at the heart of the March.

You can still camp here. You can still cut. After the Warden: *The forest kept you. It was not the road.*

### [[Silkdeep]] — The Silkdeep Caves (wrong)

**Limestone caves** under the hills, wet, loud with clicks. The Path ducks into a mouth in the rock. Cave spiders poison every bite. Giant centipedes chew armor. Walking cocoons (husks) force you to strike them first. Scorpions shoot stingers that make poison worse. Small webbers glue a weapon in your hand. The **Spider Queen** holds the lowest chamber.

A readable dungeon. After the Queen: *The silk is quiet. The Waystar still points elsewhere.*

### [[Tollroad]] — The King's Mile (**true**)

A **real road** with booths, chains, and stolen banners. Goblins stamped a fake kingdom onto it: tariffs, "guards," a king who sat down on a wagon-throne. Club goblins, cutpurses, gate brutes, archers, wagon snipers.

The **Goblin King** is the act boss. His false kingdom still taxes in the name of a power the goblins did not invent: **carnival priests** brought its seal down the Mile. He dies whispering, "Magus... you promised..." A dark pendant hidden beneath his gold then begins to shine. Touching it reveals an unknown figure who dismisses the King's wealth as children's toys and promises to bring beings capable of using the world's hidden treasures for higher purposes.

After: the apparition dissolves. The hero suspects it was the **Magus**, but the name is all they know. They keep the warm pendant, certain it will be needed later.

## Act 2 — Worse country

### [[Boneflood]] — The Ossuary Fields (wrong)

**Mass graves and open ossuaries** on a plain the old empire used as a bone-dump. The ground does not keep shut. Walking skeletons in rusted ranks, heaps that want to be one body again, crypt wardens in leftover plate, bone archers, things that throw skulls. The **Giant Skeleton** is the field's moving landmark — a war-dead colossus, not a king and not a god.

This is **quantity of dead**, not a court (that is Duskhold) and not a church of the Magus. After: *The dead have no church. This was not the house.*

### [[Brassfair]] — The Night Fair (**true**)

A **dark carnival** that should not be here: always after sundown, always the same brass lanterns, always a new field that was empty at noon. Masked attendants with prize-blades. Clockwork barkers that shout and crush. Brass imps in the sawdust. Riflemen at the shooting gallery who shoot people. Fortune-throwers lobbing cursed trinkets. Behind the main tent: the **Ringmaster**.

This is the cult the goblins learned. The fair **sells the deity** as a wonder — tickets, relics, a painted sphere. The Ringmaster is priest and fraud in one coat. He will say the tent is a porch. The house is **inside the Second Moon**.

Events already in-game: Something Wicked, The Brass Wizard; Music Box tone lives here but stays any-location.

After: *The fair sold the sky. The house is inward.*

### [[Duskhold]] — Castle Duskhold (wrong)

A **vampire castle** on a ridge. Village below pays a blood-tithe. Thralls with farm tools, fledgling vampires, armored castle knights, gargoyles off the parapet, a chanter on the balcony. The **Pale Margrave** sits the keep.

Aristocratic night. They drink the valley. They do not keep the sphere. After: *The castle drinks. It does not keep the sphere.*

## Act 3 — The sky's countries

### [[Mirrorwane]] — The Mirror Palace (wrong)

A **palace of glass and silver** that was not on any map before the sphere rose. Corridors copy the walker. You meet an Almost-You in the wrong gear. Glass constructs, shard brutes, reflections that shoot your shot back, black wells that spit tar. The **False Path** is a guide who looks like the Waystar's road and is not.

You can finish a run here. You did not meet the Magus. After: *You fought a copy. The Magus was not in the glass.*

### [[Spherefall]] — The Glass Craters (wrong)

**Impact plains**: black glass, star-iron, craters you can walk into. Scarabs in the glass, brutes clad in fallen metal, ghouls scavenging pits, gunners firing sky-shards, spitters of unstable crystal. The **Crater Crown** is the sovereign of the scar — a thing that grew in the biggest hole.

This is the Second Moon's wound **from outside**. Standing in the crater is not entering the house. After: *You stood in the wound. You did not step through.*

### [[Starfold]] — The Inner Sky (**true**)

You step **through** the wound. The Second Moon is a building: galleries along the orbit, windows onto the ordinary moon from the wrong side, stairs on a perfect curve. Servants of the working (cast TBD). The **Magus** lives here. This is the deity the goblins named and the fair sold.

After: the true ending (write with the Magus).
