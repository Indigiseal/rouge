# Brassfair

> [!info] Location — Act 2 (**true road**)
> The dark carnival. The cult the goblins learned. See [[The True Path]], [[Locations Index]].

A **night fair** that should not be here. Empty field at noon. After sundown: brass lanterns, sawdust, a fence of painted boards, music that is slightly wrong. Masks. Games whose prizes are worse than losing. Someone always smiles.

This is not cheerful decoration on a road. It is a **church with tickets**. The name the Goblin King taxes for is sold here as the greatest wonder: a painted sphere, relics, a promise that the sky can be bought in pieces.

## Place

### The Night Fair

**The Night Fair** pitches on a different meadow every dusk and leaves no wagon ruts that last. Attendants in masks cut purse and throat with the same prize-blade. Clockwork barkers — brass chests, human grins — shout the next show and walk into the crowd. Imps made of scrap hunt dropped coins in the sawdust. The shooting gallery faces the visitors. Fortune stalls throw cursed tin.

The main tent is red and too tall. **The Ringmaster** comes out in brass and cloth. Priest and con-man. He will talk. He wants an audience more than a corpse.

If the player came from Tollroad, he already knows the tax-name. If not, he still sells the same god and may mention goblins who learned it late.

After the fight: *The fair sold the sky. The house is inward.* He has pointed at [[Starfold]].
