# Boneflood

> [!info] Location — Act 2 (wrong road)
> Ossuary plains: mass graves, walking bones, a giant of war-dead. Not a church. See [[The True Path]], [[Locations Index]].

**Mass graves and open ossuaries** — a bone-dump of an old empire. The ground does not keep shut. Rank skeletons, heaps, crypt wardens, bone bows, skull-throwers. The Giant Skeleton is a walking landmark, not a king and not a god. Distinct from Duskhold: these dead flood; they do not rule a court.

## Theme

Unearthed dead — the ground returns what it was meant to keep
