# Silkdeep

> [!info] Location — Act 1 (wrong road)
> Limestone caves, spiders, a queen in the lowest chamber. Not the Waystar's road. See [[The True Path]], [[Locations Index]].

**Limestone caves** under the hills. Wet stone, silk, clicks. Cave spiders, centipedes, walking cocoons, scorpions, webbers. The Spider Queen holds the lowest chamber.

## Theme

Depth and silk — what lived in cracks climbs toward the light
