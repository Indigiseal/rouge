# Duskhold

> [!info] Location — Act 2
> One of three Act 2 roads. **Wrong path.** The castle is a real horror. It is not the god's house. See [[The True Path]], [[Locations Index]].

A **vampire castle** on a black ridge, a day's ride from the last living villages. Not a metaphor. A keep, a courtyard, a chapel with no cross left on it, cellars that smell of iron. The Margrave's court drinks the valley and calls it rent.

## Place

### Castle Duskhold

**Castle Duskhold** sits above a handful of hamlets that still farm. At dusk a cart comes down the switchback: casks, not grain. The peasants know the tithe. Some go up the hill and do not come down walking.

Inside: cold halls, red-glass windows, portraits that watch, a throne of dark wood. Bats in the rafters are not decoration. Knights in old plate still stand watch because the Margrave told them to, years after their hearts stopped being the point.

This is Act 2 because it is **aristocratic night** — worse than a goblin booth, still a place with doors and a master. It is not the Magus. The court drinks blood, not orbit. After the Margrave falls, the card is: *The castle drinks. It does not keep the sphere.*

Castle of night — vampires, thralls, and a valley that pays in pints

## Features

- Night as a rule: lanterns, long shadows, windows that do not show the Second Moon clearly (the court hates a rival light).
- Blood as economy: tithe carts, cellars, stained linen.
- Courtesy as a weapon: invitations, "guest" rooms, a table set for one.
- Distinct from Boneflood: these dead **rule**. They do not flood out of the dirt.
