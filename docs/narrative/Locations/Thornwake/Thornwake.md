# Thornwake

> [!info] Location — Act 1 (wrong road)
> The Briar March: a forest that ate the trade road. After the Warden this is not the Waystar's road. See [[The True Path]], [[Locations Index]].

A **forest that ate the old trade road**. A day's ride from the City the packed dirt disappears under briar. Wolves with thorn through the hide. Walking trees. Vine-hydras arrows pass through. Sporelings. Angry fairies. The Green Warden keeps the March.

## Theme

Living wilds — growth that refuses to stay outside the walls
